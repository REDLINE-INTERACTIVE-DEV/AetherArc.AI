# AetherArc.AI
This is the project including 4 ai that have specific tasks and specialities, one is CodingAi, one is ResearchAi, one is image/video creation and idea Ai and the last one is the ManagerAi.It works as the user talks to the manager by default, the manager gives the task tp the other ai. You can also talk to the other Ai and see their progress each.
You can also give tasks to the other ai specifically and we have a history saving and many other features.The diagram to understand it better is given below:-
